# 3. Custom ops in Mojo

MAX's operations are written in Mojo, and you can write your own the same
way. This chapter writes a kernel in a notebook cell, calls it from your
model, tests and times it, and then looks underneath `compile` at the graph
API that MAX builds models with.

| Section                                                   | You learn                                                                                                     |
|-----------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| [3.1 Your first custom op](./03_01_custom_ops.md)         | Writing a fused SwiGLU kernel in a `%%mojo` cell, calling it with `F.custom`, and measuring whether it helps. |
| [3.2 Under the hood: the graph API](./03_02_graph_api.md) | What `compile` builds, `max.graph`, and MAX's weight loaders.                                                 |

To go deeper into GPU programming in Mojo, work through the
[Mojo GPU puzzles](https://github.com/modular/mojo-gpu-puzzles). The handbook's
[kernel optimization](https://handbook.modular.com/kernel-optimization/) chapter
explains why fused kernels matter.
